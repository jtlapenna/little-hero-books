## Fresh live reproduction

The reference archive contains source and source hashes, not runnable credentials or consumed private inputs. Before a new run:

1. Fetch current main; preserve unrelated changes and prior journals. Review the actual deployed source, canonical workflow graphs and the no-payment account/key binding.
2. Verify no saved payment methods, zero/inactive wallets and no auto-top-up, plus plan/invoice/other-credit routes. Owner confirmation supplements dashboard evidence; card removal alone is insufficient. Do not fund or attach payment.
3. Make fresh <=50-character TEST-GELATO root/child identities. Freeze the current selected configuration through canonical W0. Generate visible edition/child/page/cover labels and independent PDF byte/geometry receipts. Persist only held synthetic rows; ordinary scheduling remains closed.
4. Adapt and independently review the protected runner. Bind exact source, fixture, raster/PDF and account hashes; use a durable intent journal and the shared project lock. Keep originals owner-only and ignored. Do not reuse this run's windows or webhooks.
5. Create only dedicated inactive workflow copies. Open one exact short cohort window, claim through the canonical backend, activate only the owned copy and invoke its new webhook once. Reconcile uncertain responses read-only.
6. Verify execution graph/defaults/version/input, product/edition/count/recipient/service, provider original bytes, complete provider/member mappings and authenticated admin output. Capture private raw provider responses and separate all estimates, invoices and charges.
7. Independently close authorization windows, quarantine unclaimed own rows, deactivate both dedicated copies and verify all normal controls OFF. Cleanup cannot depend on fixture/source verification or an intact main journal. Preserve ordinary-row hash/count receipts and zero customer notification intents.

Never infer current live controls from a historical receipt. Recheck immediately before any new window. Regular conversion, payment restoration and physical proof are separate operations; normal production orders must not change mode globally.
