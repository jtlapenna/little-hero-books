# Ahrefs SEO Action Plan - How to Actually Improve Rankings

**Last Updated**: November 14, 2025  
**Status**: Active Implementation Guide

---

## 🎯 Overview

This guide shows you how to use Ahrefs to actively improve your SEO rankings, not just track them. Once your Rank Tracker populates in 24 hours, you'll have data to work with. But you can start taking action now.

---

## 📊 Phase 1: Baseline Analysis (Do This Now)

### 1. Site Audit - Find Technical Issues

**Where**: Ahrefs → Site Audit

**What to Do**:
1. Add your domain: `little-hero-books.com` (or your actual domain)
2. Run a full crawl
3. Review critical issues:
   - Broken links (404 errors)
   - Missing meta descriptions
   - Missing title tags
   - Slow page speed
   - Mobile usability issues
   - Duplicate content

**Action Items**:
- Fix all critical errors first
- Fix high-priority warnings
- Address medium-priority issues over time

**Why It Matters**: Technical SEO issues prevent Google from properly indexing and ranking your pages.

---

### 2. Content Gap Analysis - Find Missing Opportunities

**Where**: Ahrefs → Content Gap

**What to Do**:
1. Enter your domain in "Target"
2. Add competitor domains:
   - wonderbly.com
   - iseeme.com
   - hoorayheroes.com
   - magicstory.com
   - magicpicturestory.com
3. Click "Show keywords"
4. Filter by:
   - Volume: >50
   - KD: <30 (for quick wins)
   - Intent: Commercial, Transactional

**Action Items**:
- Identify keywords competitors rank for that you don't
- Prioritize by volume and KD
- Create content targeting these keywords
- Focus on keywords with low KD first (easier to rank)

**Why It Matters**: This shows you exactly what content your competitors have that you're missing.

---

### 3. Backlink Analysis - See What's Working for Competitors

**Where**: Ahrefs → Site Explorer → Enter competitor domain → Backlinks

**What to Do**:
1. For each competitor, check:
   - Total backlinks
   - Referring domains
   - Top linking pages
   - Anchor text distribution
2. Look for patterns:
   - Which sites link to them?
   - What type of content gets links?
   - What anchor text do they use?

**Action Items**:
- Create similar high-quality content that earned competitors links
- Reach out to sites linking to competitors (offer better content)
- Build relationships with relevant bloggers/influencers
- Create linkable assets (guides, tools, resources)

**Why It Matters**: Backlinks are a major ranking factor. See what works for competitors and replicate it.

---

## 📈 Phase 2: Content Strategy (Start This Week)

### 4. Keyword Research for Content Creation

**Where**: Ahrefs → Keywords Explorer

**What to Do**:
1. Enter your seed keywords from your list
2. Use "Also rank for" to find related keywords
3. Filter by:
   - Volume: 50-500 (sweet spot for new sites)
   - KD: <25 (achievable difficulty)
   - Intent: Match your content goal

**Action Items**:
- Create one piece of content per week targeting these keywords
- Focus on keywords with low KD but decent volume
- Create content that answers user intent
- Include your target keyword naturally in:
  - Title tag
  - H1
  - First paragraph
  - URL slug
  - Meta description

**Why It Matters**: Targeting the right keywords with the right difficulty level helps you rank faster.

---

### 5. SERP Analysis - See What Google Wants

**Where**: Ahrefs → Keywords Explorer → Enter keyword → Click "SERP" button

**What to Do**:
1. For each target keyword, analyze the SERP:
   - What type of content ranks? (blog posts, product pages, guides)
   - What's the average word count?
   - What questions appear in "People Also Ask"?
   - What featured snippets exist?
   - What schema markup is used?

**Action Items**:
- Match the content type that ranks (if blogs rank, write a blog)
- Aim for similar or better word count
- Answer the "People Also Ask" questions in your content
- Target featured snippets with concise, well-structured answers
- Add relevant schema markup

**Why It Matters**: Google shows you exactly what it wants to rank. Give it that.

---

### 6. Content Ideas from Top Competitors

**Where**: Ahrefs → Content Explorer

**What to Do**:
1. Search for topics like:
   - "personalized children's book"
   - "custom kids book"
   - "photo storybook"
2. Filter by:
   - Top pages (most backlinks)
   - Recent (published in last 6 months)
   - High traffic potential
3. Analyze top-performing content:
   - What topics do they cover?
   - What angle do they take?
   - What makes them successful?

**Action Items**:
- Create better versions of top-performing content
- Cover topics competitors haven't addressed
- Add unique value (better examples, more detail, updated info)
- Create content that fills gaps in competitor coverage

**Why It Matters**: See what content actually performs, then create something better.

## 🔗 Phase 3: Link Building (Ongoing)

### 7. Find Link Opportunities

**Where**: Ahrefs → Content Explorer → Filter by "Broken links" or "Mentions"

**What to Do**:
1. Search for your competitors' brand names
2. Find pages mentioning them
3. Check for broken links on those pages
4. Offer your content as a replacement

**Action Items**:
- Create a spreadsheet of link opportunities
- Reach out to site owners with broken links
- Offer your content as a better alternative
- Build relationships with relevant sites

**Why It Matters**: Broken link building is one of the easiest ways to get quality backlinks.

---

### 8. Monitor Brand Mentions

**Where**: Ahrefs → Alerts → Set up brand mention alerts

**What to Do**:
1. Set up alerts for:
   - "Little Hero Books"
   - "personalized children's book"
   - Your competitors' brand names
2. Check mentions weekly
3. Reach out when appropriate

**Action Items**:
- Thank people who mention you
- Ask for links when appropriate
- Engage with discussions about your niche
- Build relationships with people talking about your industry

**Why It Matters**: Brand mentions without links are missed opportunities.

---

## 📊 Phase 4: Ongoing Monitoring & Optimization (Weekly/Monthly)

### 9. Rank Tracker Analysis (Once Data Populates)

**Where**: Ahrefs → Rank Tracker → Your project

**What to Do** (Weekly):
1. Review ranking changes:
   - Which keywords improved?
   - Which keywords dropped?
   - Any new rankings?
2. Identify patterns:
   - Did content updates help?
   - Did competitors outrank you?
   - Are seasonal trends affecting rankings?
3. Take action:
   - Optimize pages that dropped
   - Build on pages that improved
   - Create content for keywords you're close to ranking for

**Action Items**:
- Weekly review of top 10 Rank Tracker keywords
- Monthly review of all 100 keywords
- Document what works and what doesn't
- Adjust strategy based on data

**Why It Matters**: Rankings change constantly. Regular monitoring helps you catch opportunities and problems early.

---

### 10. Competitor Monitoring

**Where**: Ahrefs → Site Explorer → Competitor domains → Organic keywords

**What to Do** (Monthly):
1. Check what new keywords competitors are ranking for
2. See which pages are gaining/losing traffic
3. Monitor their new content
4. Track their backlink growth

**Action Items**:
- Replicate successful competitor strategies
- Create content for keywords they're targeting
- Build links from sites linking to them
- Stay ahead of their content strategy

**Why It Matters**: Competitors are doing SEO too. Monitor them to stay competitive.

---

### 11. Traffic Analysis

**Where**: Ahrefs → Site Explorer → Your domain → Organic keywords

**What to Do** (Monthly):
1. Review your top traffic-driving keywords
2. Identify pages getting the most organic traffic
3. See which keywords are bringing conversions (if you have conversion tracking)
4. Find keywords with high traffic but low conversions

**Action Items**:
- Optimize high-traffic pages for conversions
- Create more content around successful topics
- Fix pages with high traffic but low engagement
- Double down on what's working

**Why It Matters**: Not all traffic is equal. Focus on traffic that converts.

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with Low-Hanging Fruit**: Target keywords with KD < 25 first. You'll see results faster.

2. **Content Quality > Quantity**: One great piece of content that ranks is better than 10 mediocre pieces.

3. **Monitor Regularly**: SEO is ongoing. Weekly monitoring catches issues early.

4. **Learn from Competitors**: They've done the research. See what works for them and do it better.

5. **Track Everything**: Document what works. Build on successes, learn from failures.

6. **Be Patient**: SEO takes time. Rankings improve gradually, not overnight.

7. **Focus on User Intent**: Create content that actually helps users, not just for SEO.

8. **Build Relationships**: Link building is relationship building. Be helpful, not spammy.
