# Shot list — 3 photos for IG launch

You can shoot all of these in **one ~30-minute session** with your phone. No studio, no DSLR. Daylight + a clean surface is everything.

## Universal rules

- **Time of day:** late morning or mid-afternoon. Avoid harsh midday sun and golden-hour orange.
- **Light direction:** window to your *side* (90°), not behind the camera (flat) or behind the subject (silhouette).
- **Weather:** overcast > clear sky. Soft diffuse light is more forgiving than direct sun.
- **Phone settings:** turn HDR on. Lock exposure on the book/face by tap-and-hold. Shoot in 1:1 (square) or in 4:3 and crop later.
- **Take 5-10 of each.** The 30-second variance between exposures, expressions, and angles is where the keeper hides.

---

## Shot 1 — Book on a soft surface (Post 1)

**Goal:** "This is a real, beautiful object." Cover-forward, calm, premium.

**Surface:**
- Linen tea towel (off-white or oatmeal)
- Or kraft paper (a roll from any craft store)
- Or unfinished light wood
- **Avoid:** glossy surfaces, busy patterns, pure-white photo backdrops (reads sterile)

**Composition:**
- Book square in frame, slightly off-center (rule of thirds)
- One small accessory at the edge — a single autumn leaf, a sprig of eucalyptus, a wooden block, a kid's small toy. Optional, only if it fits naturally.
- **Avoid:** props that compete with the book (other books, mugs, coffee, plants too leafy)

**Camera angle:**
- Top-down 90° flat-lay (cleanest)
- Or 75° (slight tilt) if you want a more dimensional feel

**3 variations to capture:**
1. Flat-lay, book centered, no hands
2. Same setup, hand resting on the cover (yours or your husband's)
3. Book held by a child's hands (if your kid is around) — highest emotional pull

**Watch out for:**
- Phone shadow falling across the book (move yourself or window)
- Flash — never use it
- Reflections on the cover from glossy lamination

## Shot 2 — Founder note (Post 4)

**Goal:** "Real human runs this. Not a brand." Warm, lived-in, founder-direct.

**Setup:**
- Living room, kitchen counter, or porch — somewhere that reads "home"
- Neutral background (wall, bookshelf, simple kitchen tile). **Avoid:** TV in frame, cluttered counters, bright primary colors.
- Daylight from a window beside the camera (90° to your face), not behind you

**3 variations to capture:**
1. **Solo, holding the book** — chest-level, looking at the camera, slight smile (not toothy). Frame from upper chest to top of head.
2. **You + husband together, both holding the book** — shoulders touching, both looking at camera or both looking at the book together. The "together looking at the book" is more emotionally honest.
3. **Hands-only** — the two of you both holding the book at chest level, faces cropped out. Use this if a face shot feels too much for v1; you can always swap it later when you're ready.

**What to wear:**
- Solid earthy color (cream, sage, navy, rust). No logos, no patterns.
- Whatever you'd genuinely wear — don't dress up. The whole point is "this is a small studio run by real people."

**Watch out for:**
- Backlight (window behind you turns you into a silhouette)
- Phone too close — chest-up framing is much more flattering than face-only
- Forced smile — neutral or soft is more believable

## Shot 3 — Open spread, top-down (Post 8)

**Goal:** "Look at the craft." Show watercolor detail and the personalization in scene.

**Setup:**
- Same surface options as Shot 1 (linen / kraft / light wood)
- Pick a spread that has the personalized hero clearly visible — the forest run, the beach, the mountain vista, the magic doorway. **Avoid** the cover (already covered by Shot 1) and the dedication page (looks empty).
- Open the book flat. If it doesn't sit flat, gently weight the corners with two small smooth stones or wooden blocks (kept just out of frame).

**Camera angle:**
- 90° top-down, dead vertical
- Phone needs to be parallel to the page — even a few degrees tilt distorts the spread
- Use both hands to steady, or rest your forearms on a chair back to stop sway

**3 variations to capture:**
1. **Full spread, both pages visible** — book centered, even margins around all four edges
2. **One-page focus** — zoom in on the page with the most beautiful watercolor detail
3. **Spread + thumb** — capture a corner with your thumb just touching the page edge for human scale

**Watch out for:**
- Your shadow falling across the page (turn the book or move yourself relative to the window)
- Glare from window directly onto the page (rotate the book until the glare is off)
- Curved page bow — flatten the spread before shooting; tape lightly out of frame if needed

---

## After the shoot

1. Pick the best 1 from each shot (the variations let you choose later).
2. Crop to **1:1 square** at 1080×1080 minimum.
3. Light edit:
   - **Brightness +5 to +10** (most phone shots are slightly under)
   - **Warmth +3 to +8** (matches the LHL palette better)
   - **Sharpness +5** if the watercolor detail looks soft
   - **Avoid filters.** No VSCO presets, no "moody" or "vibrant" filters. The watercolor + linen does the work.
4. Save and add to `docs/marketing/instagram-launch/posts/` as `post-1-cover.jpg`, `post-4-founder.jpg`, `post-8-spread-detail.jpg`.

You don't need a perfectionist eye — "good and authentic" beats "perfect and stiff" for this audience every time.
