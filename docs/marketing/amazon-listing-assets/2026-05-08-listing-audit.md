# Amazon Listing Audit: B0G4QPLWKH

**Audited:** 2026-05-08 (signed-in shopper view, desktop)
**ASIN:** [B0G4QPLWKH](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G4QPLWKH)
**Current state:** 5.0 stars (10 reviews) | $29.99 | BSR #1,055,974 in Books

This is a gap analysis between the live listing, the [v2 plan](../../new-planning/marketing/lhl_amazon_listing_title_bullets_a_image_captions_v_2.md), and the research-backed best practices for personalized children's books. P0 items are broken or factually wrong. P1 items are the highest-leverage upgrades. P2 is polish.

---

## TL;DR — the 6 highest-leverage moves

1. **Fix the wrong category.** Listing currently ranks in *Stress Management Self-Help* (#4,533) and *Children's Books on Health* (#4,559). Neither is where parents shopping for personalized books look. Move to *Children's Books → Growing Up & Facts of Life* or *Children's Books → Personalized Books*.
2. **Add 5 more carousel images.** Currently only 4 of 9 slots used. The missing 5 slots are the highest-converting ones: differentiator showcase, customization range, "how it works", gift lifestyle, and "what you'll receive."
3. **Upload a product video.** The Videos section is empty (literally invites *us* to upload). Research: ~9.7% sales lift just from adding video.
4. **Rewrite the title and description.** Current title uses 156 of 200 allowed characters and never says "hair," "skin tone," "animal companion," or "hometown." Description leads with cliché, uses corporate fluff, has a wrong age range, and is one giant block.
5. **Fix the factual errors.** Page says "Ages 1-7" (brand truth: 0-7) and "17 pages" (brand truth: 16 — 14 interior + covers). These flow into search filters and filter buyers out.
6. **Harvest the customer photos and pull testimonials.** Six+ customer-uploaded photos exist in "Reviews with images." Five verified-purchase reviews contain quotable testimonials. None of this content is being reused on the listing or in marketing.

## What's currently on the page

### Title (156 chars, 44 chars unused)
> Little Hero Labs Personalized Children's Book, Finding Our Inner Voice, Self-Discovery Adventure Story for Ages 1-7, Customizable Character and Name

**Issues:**
- Leads with brand name, not the keyword shoppers search ("personalized children's book").
- Says "Ages 1-7" instead of brand-truth "Ages 0-7."
- Doesn't include the differentiating keywords: *hair style, skin tone, hometown, animal companion, pronouns*. These are search terms parents type.
- "Customizable Character and Name" undersells the actual customization (11 fields).

### Description (single block, no formatting)
> Embark on a magical journey of self-discovery with this personalized children's book from Little Hero Labs. Designed for ages 1-7 [...] This thoughtfully crafted tale serves as both an engaging bedtime story and a gentle tool for discussing feelings and self-trust with young children. Create a cherished keepsake that will inspire your child to make kind and brave choices while developing their emotional intelligence.

**Issues:**
- Opens with "Embark on a magical journey" — generic kids-book cliché.
- "Thoughtfully crafted tale," "emotional intelligence," "cherished keepsake" — corporate fluff. Brand voice rules call this out.
- Wrong age range repeated.
- Never names the actual customization options. A parent reading this can't tell whether it's just-the-name personalization or full character personalization.
- No founder/origin angle ("made by two dads in California").
- One paragraph block. No visual breaks. No bulleted "About this item" structure.

#### P1-B: Rewrite description with "About this item" bullets

Amazon allows formatted bullets via HTML in the description for books. Lead with differentiator, then customization range, then story/theme, then specs, then founder/origin, then gift framing. Five bullets, each ≤2 sentences. Pull verbatim from the [v2 plan](../../new-planning/marketing/lhl_amazon_listing_title_bullets_a_image_captions_v_2.md#key-product-features-bullets) — that work is already done. The current description ignores it.

Keep a 1-paragraph hook at the top (≤60 words), then the bullets. Cut "embark on a magical journey," "thoughtfully crafted tale," "emotional intelligence."

## What this fixes

If P0 + P1 ship as planned:

- **Discoverability:** correct category + better title keywords + fuller backend search terms = more impressions.
- **Conversion at the listing:** 9-image carousel with differentiator, range, process, and lifestyle slots covers every research-backed buyer objection. Video adds another ~10% lift.
- **Buyer trust:** factual fixes (age, page count) stop the listing being filtered out by category browsers using filters. Customer photos + reviewer quotes (when surfaced as image overlays) are the strongest social proof we have.
- **Voice consistency:** rewritten title and description align with the v2 plan that's already drafted but never shipped.

---

## What this doesn't fix

These need Brand Registry (which needs the trademark Jeff can't fund yet):

- A+ Content (no headline modules, no comparison chart, no founder module).
- Amazon Stores page.
- Sponsored Brands ads.
- Amazon Attribution tracking.

The existing `aplus-modules/` folder stays parked until trademark resolves. The work in there isn't wasted — when Brand Registry unlocks, the modules ship. Until then, the carousel + video + description carry the entire listing.
