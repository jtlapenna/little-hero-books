## Design specs

**Format:**
- 1 page, US Letter (8.5" × 11") or A4
- PDF output
- Print-safe at 300dpi (recipients may print)

**Visual direction:**
- Warm watercolor / storybook aesthetic
- Brand colors from `frontend/src/styles/global.css` (Hero Coral, Golden Yellow, Sky Blue, Sage Green, Soft Teal, Storybook Beige)
- Garamond for body, Poppins for any UI-feeling labels (per existing design system)
- Soft drop shadows or paper-texture background, not flat corporate
- Generous whitespace; this is a one-pager, not a brochure

**Image treatment:**
- No heavy text overlays on hero image
- Cover image gets the most visual weight
- Lifestyle photo placed lower; smaller; conveys "real product, real families"

**Anti-patterns:**
- No stock-photo "happy family" energy
- No corporate gradient backgrounds
- No emoji decoration in headings
- No marketing buzzwords ("revolutionary," "game-changing," "delightful")

**Output:** save final PDF to `docs/marketing/outreach/lhl/assets/press-kit-v1.pdf` (gitignored, since binary). Source design file (Figma, Affinity, etc.) at `docs/marketing/outreach/lhl/assets/press-kit-source/`.
