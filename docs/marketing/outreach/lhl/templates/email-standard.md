# Email template

Reflects the locked voice. See [`voice-guide.md`](../voice-guide.md) and [`anti-patterns.md`](../anti-patterns.md).

## How to use

- Use email as the **primary channel** whenever a public email or contact form exists. Higher response rate than DMs and no rate-limit pressure.
- Aim 100–180 words. Long enough to be clear, short enough to actually get read.
- **Default opener:** direct founder intro. Specific-reference openers go in A/B variants only.
- Sign as **Jeff / Little Hero Labs** with the URL.
- Include the sample-preview link: `https://www.littleherolabs.com/preview` (one-page visual walkthrough, noindex, dedicated for outreach).
- Run [humanizer](../../../../.claude/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md) on every batch before Jeff reviews.
- No em dashes. Periods, commas, parentheses, colons only.
- Paragraph break every 1–3 sentences.

## Subject line options

Pick based on the creator's tone. All sentence case, no em dashes, no clickbait.

- "Gifted book pitch for [Channel Name]"
- "Personalized kids book, gifted collab?"
- "Quick partnership pitch from Little Hero Labs"
- "A children's book where your kid is the hero, gifted collab?"
- "Pitching for an upcoming gift roundup" *(when pitching editorial mention vs personal review)*

## Default template (gifted collab pitch, ~140 words)

```
Hi [Name],

I'm Jeff, founder of Little Hero Labs. We make personalized picture books where your child becomes the hero of the story, illustrated to look like them, with their favorite animal joining for the final reveal.

We're running partner campaigns this quarter and looking for a few more creators. Would you be open to a gifted collab? I'd send you a personalized copy for your family, no commitment beyond your honest reaction.

If you like the book, I'll share more about our campaign offers.

You can see what the book looks like here: https://www.littleherolabs.com/preview

Jeff
Little Hero Labs | littleherolabs.com
```

## A/B variants (one variable at a time; see voice-guide §A/B testing)

### Variant A1: specific-reference opener

Use when piloting the opener-style A/B test. Reference must be **actually specific** to the creator's content, not generic praise. Keep it to one sentence, then pivot.

```
Hi [Name],

[Specific reference: a recent post, a named episode, a recurring theme.] I'm Jeff, founder of Little Hero Labs.

We make personalized picture books where your child becomes the hero of the story, illustrated to look like them, with their favorite animal joining for the final reveal.

We're running partner campaigns this quarter and looking for a few more creators. Would you be open to a gifted collab? I'd send you a personalized copy for your family, no commitment beyond your honest reaction.

If you like the book, I'll share more about our campaign offers.

You can see what the book looks like here: https://www.littleherolabs.com/preview

Jeff
Little Hero Labs | littleherolabs.com
```

### Variant B1: Wonderbly named

Use when piloting the comparison A/B test. The default is no naming; this variant is for the explicit-comparison test only. See [voice-guide §competitor mentions](../voice-guide.md).

## Campaign-angle adjustment

Once the opener and offer mention are locked, tweak the middle to match the creator's natural format. The angle goes between the founder intro and the partnership ask.

| Format | Inserted middle line |
|---|---|
| Read-aloud / storytime | "Your audience already gathers around your read-alouds. Could be a natural fit." |
| Unboxing / reaction | "The first-reaction moment when a kid sees themselves on the cover tends to be unreal on camera." |
| Gift guide | "It's a strong pick for a personalized-gift roundup. Genuinely the most differentiated thing in the category right now." |
| Amazon finds | "It's already live on Amazon Custom, so your audience can buy directly from where you point them." |
| Montessori / educational | "The story is about a child finding their own inner voice. Aligned with what your audience already cares about." |
| Grandparent | "Grandparent-approved in 3 taps. Created on Amazon, typically arrives in 3–5 business days." |
| Pregnancy / baby shower | "A memorable first-birthday gift. The book is for ages 0–7, so it grows with the kid." |
| Diverse / inclusive parenting | "Every child sees a hero who actually looks like them. That representation lands harder for audiences that haven't had that in books before." |
| Substack / newsletter | "Could be a fit for an upcoming gift roundup or a sponsored issue." |
| Podcast | "Open to discussing sponsorship for a host read or a 30-second segment." |

If a creator's format doesn't map to the table, propose the most authentic fit and flag for Jeff's approval.

## Constraints

- No em dashes anywhere. Re-read every draft.
- No fabricated social proof, partner names, or sales numbers.
- Never name a dollar amount in the first email. Compensation tiers go in a follow-up after expressed interest.
- Sign as Jeff, never an AI persona, never "we" alone.
- Run humanizer on the full batch before presenting to Jeff.
- Paragraph breaks every 1–3 sentences. No walls of text.
