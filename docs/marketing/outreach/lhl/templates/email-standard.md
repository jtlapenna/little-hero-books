# Standard email template (175–250 words)

## How to use

- Use email as the primary channel whenever a public email or contact form exists. Higher response rates and no rate-limit pressure vs DMs.
- Sign as **Jeff / Little Hero Labs**.
- Include the sample-preview link if one exists (asset phase output). Without a preview, the email is meaningfully less effective — pitch should mention "happy to share a preview if helpful."
- Run [humanizer](../../../../.claude/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md) on every batch before Jeff approves.

## Subject line options

- "Gifted collab? Personalized kids book — your audience is a perfect fit"
- "Quick partnership idea for [Channel Name]"
- "A children's book where your kid is the hero — collaboration?"
- "Could we send you a book for [child's name]?" *(only if child's name is publicly mentioned by the creator)*
- "Your readers / viewers would love this — gifted collab?"

Pick based on the creator's tone. Avoid generic subject lines.

## Body template

```
Hi [Name],

I'm Jeff, the founder of Little Hero Labs. We create beautifully illustrated personalized children's books — but here's what makes us different from Wonderbly and similar: instead of picking a pre-made character, parents create their own. Their child's actual hair style, skin tone, favorite color, and animal sidekick — that's the hero on every page of the story.

I've been following your [specific content type — storytime / gift guides / Montessori content / parenting reels] for a while. [One specific, genuine observation about a recent post, a theme they consistently hit, or what their audience clearly responds to.]

We're on Amazon Custom and looking for a few creators to share a [matched format — name-reveal unboxing / natural read-aloud / gift-guide mention / reaction video] of the personalized book in their content.

The ask is simple to start:
- I send you a free personalized book for your family
- If you love it, we'd love to hear about it

If the fit feels right after that, we can talk about a simple partnership structure (custom discount code revenue share, or a small flat fee — your choice).

[Optional, if asset exists:] You can see what the book looks like here: [Sample preview URL]

Happy to send more details or answer questions — just reply here.

Thanks,
Jeff
Little Hero Labs | littleherolabs.com
```

## Campaign angle matching

Match the angle in the email body to the creator's natural format:

| Creator format | Angle to propose |
|---|---|
| Read-aloud / storytime | Natural read-along — "show the spreads, share a child's reaction to seeing themselves" |
| Unboxing / reaction | Kid's first reaction to seeing themselves on the cover |
| Gift guide | "Best personalized gift for [age range] — it's the only one where you create the character" |
| Amazon finds | "Found this on Amazon Custom — has to be seen to believe the personalization" |
| Montessori / educational | "Story is about a child finding their inner voice — emotional intelligence theme" |
| Grandparent | "Grandparent-approved in 3 taps — Amazon checkout, arrives in 3–5 days" |
| Pregnancy / baby shower | "Memorable first-birthday gift" |
| Diverse / inclusive parenting | Lead with the representation angle: "every child sees a hero who actually looks like them" |
| Substack / newsletter | "Could be a great fit for an upcoming gift roundup or a sponsored issue" |
| Podcast | "Sponsorship interest — host read or 30-second segment mention" |

If a creator's format doesn't match a listed angle, propose the most authentic fit and flag for Jeff's approval.

## Pitch principles (mirror of DM principles, applied to email)

1. **Open with something real and specific.** Reference a real post.
2. **Lead with the gifted book.** Compensation is paragraph 3 at earliest.
3. **Show, don't tell, the differentiation.** "We let parents create their own character" beats "highly personalized."
4. **Don't claim sales numbers** unless they're verified.
5. **Match the ask to their format.** Specific format = specific propose; let them counter if they want a different format.
6. **Include a preview link** when one exists. Visual asset > paragraph of description.
7. **Keep it under 250 words.** Long emails get scanned, not read.
