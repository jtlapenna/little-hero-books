# TikTok DM template

Reflects the locked voice. See [`voice-guide.md`](../voice-guide.md) and [`anti-patterns.md`](../anti-patterns.md).

## How to use

- TikTok DMs hit harder when shorter than Instagram. Aim 60–90 words.
- Pre-engagement (24h prior, like 1–2 posts + leave one genuine comment) is required for TikTok DMs.
- TikTok daily DM cap: **3 max**, well-spaced. Stop at the first warning the platform gives you.
- Sign as **Jeff / Little Hero Labs**.
- No em dashes. Use periods, commas, parentheses, colons.
- Run [humanizer](../../../../.claude/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md) on every batch before Jeff reviews.

## Default template (~75 words)

```
Hi [Name], Jeff here from Little Hero Labs.

We make personalized kids' books where your child becomes the hero of the story, illustrated to look like them.

We're running partner campaigns this quarter. Open to a gifted collab? I'd send a personalized copy for your family, no commitment beyond your honest reaction.

If you like the book, I'll share more about our campaign offers.

Jeff
Little Hero Labs | littleherolabs.com
```

## When to use TikTok vs Instagram DMs

- **TikTok** if their primary content is on TikTok (Reels cross-posted from TikTok still count as Instagram-first, usually).
- **Instagram DMs** generally have higher response rates for product partnerships at the nano/micro tier.
- **Email** wins over both when an email is in bio or contact card. Default to email when available.

## Constraints

- TikTok daily cap: 3 DMs max, ~30+ minutes apart.
- Skip TikTok DMs if Jeff's account is new (under 6 months old or under ~100 followers). The message reads as spammy from a fresh account.
- Never click a link a creator sends back. Verify the URL first or ask them to email instead.
- Same humanizer + no-em-dash rules as the Instagram template.
