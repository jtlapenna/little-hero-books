# TikTok DM template (short — under 100 words)

## How to use

- TikTok DMs hit harder when shorter than Instagram. Aim 60–100 words.
- More casual, less formal phrasing than IG.
- Pre-engagement (24h prior — like 1–2 posts, comment once) is required for TikTok DMs. See `lhl-creator-pipeline` SKILL.md.
- Sign as **Jeff / Little Hero Labs**.
- TikTok daily DM cap: 3 max, well-spaced. Stop at the first warning.

## Template

```
Hi [Name] — Jeff here, founder of Little Hero Labs. We make personalized kids' books where the child becomes the hero — their actual look, their animal sidekick, every page.

[One specific, real comment on their content — a recent video, a recurring theme, an audience they clearly serve well.]

Open to a gifted collab? I'd send you a personalized book for your family, no strings — just love to see your honest reaction.

If you want to see what it looks like first, happy to send a preview.

— Jeff
Little Hero Labs
```

## When to use TikTok vs Instagram DMs

- TikTok if their primary content is on TikTok (Reels cross-posted from TikTok still counts as Instagram-first, usually).
- Instagram DMs generally have higher response rates for product partnerships at the nano/micro tier; TikTok favors creator-network style outreach. Default to email when both exist.

## After Jeff approves

Run humanizer over the batch. TikTok-style copy is especially prone to AI-slop tells (overly casual, em-dashes, "—".

## Notes

- Never pitch a creator who has publicly stated "no brand DMs" or has a recent post complaining about cold outreach.
- Skip TikTok DMs entirely if Jeff's TikTok account is new (under 6 months old or under ~100 followers) — the message will look spammy.
