# Instagram DM template

Reflects the locked voice. See [`voice-guide.md`](../voice-guide.md) for principles and [`anti-patterns.md`](../anti-patterns.md) for what to avoid.

## How to use

- **Default opener:** direct founder intro. Don't lead with a comment about the creator's content unless we're explicitly running the specific-reference A/B variant.
- Keep it under 150 words. Two short paragraphs is plenty.
- Pre-engagement (24h prior, like 1–2 posts + leave one genuine comment) is required for IG DMs.
- Sign as **Jeff / Little Hero Labs**. No "Echo," no AI persona, no "we" hiding the writer.
- No em dashes. Use periods, commas, parentheses, colons.
- Run [humanizer](../../../../.claude/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md) on every batch before Jeff reviews.

## A/B variants (test one variable at a time; see voice-guide §A/B testing)

### Variant A1: specific-reference opener

Use when piloting the opener-style A/B test. Reference must be **actually specific** to the creator's content, not generic praise. One sentence, then pivot.

```
Hi [Name], your [specific recent thing, e.g. a recent post, a recurring theme, a named episode] caught my attention. I'm Jeff, founder of Little Hero Labs.

We make personalized picture books where your child becomes the hero of the story, illustrated to look like them. We're running partner campaigns this quarter and looking for a few more creators. Would you be open to a gifted collab? I'd send you a personalized copy for your family, no commitment beyond your honest reaction.

If you like the book, I'll share more about our campaign offers. Quick visual walkthrough: littleherolabs.com/preview

Jeff
Little Hero Labs | littleherolabs.com
```

### Variant B1: Wonderbly named

Use when piloting the comparison A/B test.


## Variants by creator format

When you need to adjust the angle to match how they actually create (instead of changing the opener), tweak the second paragraph. Examples:

| Format | Adjusted middle line |
|---|---|
| Read-aloud / storytime | "Could be a natural read-along. Your kid sees themselves on every spread." |
| Unboxing / reaction | "The first reaction when they see themselves on the cover tends to be the moment. The story itself is gentle and quiet, about hearing your inner voice." |
| Gift guide | "Genuinely the best personalized gift I've found for this age." |
| Amazon finds | "It's on Amazon Custom. Your kid is literally the hero of the story." |
| Montessori / educational | "The story is about a child finding their own inner voice. Feels aligned with what your audience already cares about." |
| Grandparent | "Grandparent-approved in 3 taps. Created on Amazon, arrives in 3–5 days." |
| Pregnancy / baby shower | "A memorable first-birthday gift. The book is for ages 0–7." |

## Constraints

- No em dashes anywhere. Re-read every draft.
- No fabricated social proof, partner names, or sales numbers.
- Never name a dollar amount in the first DM.
- Sign as Jeff, never an AI persona.
- Run humanizer before Jeff reviews the batch.
