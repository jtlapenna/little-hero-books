# Instagram DM template (short — 100–140 words)

## How to use

- Open with one specific, real observation about the creator's content. Reference a recent post or theme.
- Match the campaign angle to the creator's natural format (see angle-matching below).
- Lead with the gifted book offer only. Compensation comes later, after interest is shown.
- Keep tone warm and founder-direct. No corporate fluff, no AI persona.
- Sign as **Jeff / Little Hero Labs** (no Echo, no AI signoff).
- Do not include rate amounts or bonus tiers in the initial DM. That conversation comes after they reply.

## Template

```
Hi [Name] — I'm Jeff, the founder of Little Hero Labs. We make personalized children's books where your child is literally the hero — their actual hair style, skin tone, favorite color, and animal sidekick on every page.

[One specific, genuine observation about their work — a recent post, a theme they consistently hit, the way they talk to their audience.]

Would you be open to a gifted collab? I'd love to send you a personalized book for your family — no commitment beyond sharing your honest reaction if you love it. If the fit feels right, we can talk about a simple partnership structure from there.

Happy to send a preview if you want to see it first.

— Jeff
Little Hero Labs
```

## Variants by creator format

| Creator format | Adjusted opener / angle |
|---|---|
| Read-aloud / storytime | "This would make a great natural read-along — the kid sees themselves in every spread." |
| Unboxing / reaction | "The first reaction when they see their own hair and animal sidekick on the cover is the moment." |
| Gift guide | "Genuinely the best personalized gift I've found for this age." |
| Amazon finds | "It's on Amazon Custom — you create your own character, not pick from pre-made ones like other personalized books." |
| Montessori / educational | "The story is about a child finding their own inner voice — feels aligned with what your audience already cares about." |
| Grandparent | "Grandparent-approved in 3 taps — created on Amazon, arrives in 3–5 days." |
| Pregnancy / baby shower | "Could be a memorable first-birthday gift — the book is for ages 0–7." |

## Pitch principles

1. **Open with something real.** Generic openers signal template.
2. **Make the audience connection explicit.** "Your followers would love this" isn't enough — say *why* they buy gifts like this.
3. **Match the ask to their format.** Don't pitch an unboxing to someone who never does unboxings.
4. **Lead with the gifted book.** Removes friction. Partnership details come later.
5. **Sound like a real founder, not a campaign.** Jeff is a small intentional studio — that story is an asset.
6. **No rate amounts in initial DM.** Always present compensation to Jeff for approval before mentioning numbers.
7. **No fabricated social proof.** If you reference past partnerships or sales, they must be real and verified.
