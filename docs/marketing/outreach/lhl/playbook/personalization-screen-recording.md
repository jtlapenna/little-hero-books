# Personalization screen recording — Storyboard

A short screen capture of the customize flow on the D2C site. Adds visual proof of "you actually create the character" — the differentiator made tangible. Use as embedded clip in DMs, IG/TikTok content briefs, and as the opening 5 seconds of the 30-second product video.

**Length target:** 12–18 seconds (under 20s). Easier for influencers to repurpose verbatim.

**Source:** D2C site (`littleherolabs.com/create/character`). The D2C flow looks better than Amazon Custom for visual capture (Amazon's UI is functional but visually constrained).

---

## Setup

**Tools (pick one):**
- macOS: QuickTime → File → New Screen Recording → record area
- Cross-platform: OBS Studio (free)
- Cleanest output: ScreenStudio (paid, auto-zooms cursor, smooth)

**Browser settings:**
- Hide bookmarks bar
- Use a clean Chrome profile (no extensions in the screenshot)
- Browser zoom: 100%
- Window size: 1280×800 (or 1440×900 for retina). Avoid full-screen, looks cleaner with browser chrome visible but minimal.
- Light theme

**Recording settings:**
- 60fps for smooth cursor motion
- 1080p output (downscale at edit time if needed)
- Capture audio: no system audio, no mic (we'll add audio in edit)

## Storyboard (shot-by-shot, ~14 seconds total)

| Time | Action | What's on screen | Pacing note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:01 | Page already loaded on `/create/character`. Cursor visible, hovering near the name field. | Empty name input, neutral character preview on the side. | Static beat — let the start register. |

| 0:05–0:07 | Click hair color, pick "dark-brown." | Character preview updates color. | Same quick beat. |
| 0:07–0:09 | Click skin tone, pick "tan." | Preview updates. | Same. |
| 0:09–0:11 | Click favorite color, pick "blue." Then click animal guide, pick "tiger." | Preview character now has chosen accent color and animal companion shown nearby. | Two clicks fairly quick. |
| 0:11–0:13 | Cursor hovers over the "Continue" or "Preview" button. | Final personalized character on screen. | Hold so the viewer sees the result. |
| 0:13–0:14 | Cut to a single still: the cover preview with the personalized name. | Cover with "Mateo's Inner Voice" or "Aria's Inner Voice" — child looking like the chosen options. | Final hold beat. |

## Voiceover decision

**Default: no voiceover.** Captions only. Cleaner for repurposing by influencers (they can speak over it themselves). Also more on-brand (warm + intentional, not narrated like an ad).

**Captions to overlay (on-screen text):**
- **0:01:** "Type their name."
- **0:03:** "Pick their look."
- **0:11:** "And their animal sidekick."
- **0:13:** "Their story. Their hero."

Captions in Poppins, large (~36pt at 1080p), centered or lower-third, white with subtle drop shadow.

**If voiceover is preferred (alternative):**
- Founder voice (Jeff), 1 sentence: *"Type their name. Pick their look. Their story, with them as the hero."*
- Tone: warm, not announcer-style.
- Recorded clean, then mixed below caption-pacing.

---

## Audio bed

- **None for the silent caption-only version.** Posted natively to social platforms, it'll auto-play silent and the captions carry the meaning.
- For embedded use (web, press kit, video), add a soft instrumental music bed. Royalty-free, gentle (think Studio Ghibli-ish or warm acoustic). Recommend Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or PremiumBeat.

---

## Editing notes

- Trim any cursor wobble or accidental hover.
- Smooth out cursor motion if the recording app didn't (in Final Cut, Premiere, or DaVinci Resolve, you can stabilize cursor paths).
- Add a 0.5s fade-in from white at the start.
- End on the final still for a beat (~1s) before fading to white.
- Export as **MP4 H.264, 1080p, 30fps** (downscaled from 60fps source for smaller file size while retaining smoothness).
- Also export a **9:16 vertical version** for IG Reels / TikTok (crop the browser window to the personalization area + cover preview side-by-side; or stack vertically).
