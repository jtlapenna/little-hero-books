# Asset / Page Map Pass 01

## Purpose

This is a first-pass map of the expanded 30-page interior, with story function, likely imagery, reuse opportunities, and pose implications.

This is not final copy and not a final beat map. The goal is to understand whether the emerging story can reuse existing assets, where new assets are truly needed, and which pages can avoid new personalized child poses.

## Working Assumptions

- Target is 30 interior pages total.
- Interior includes title page, dedication, narrative pages, and end matter.
- Cover is not counted here.
- Existing dream-world assets should be reused where they still serve the stronger story.
- New real-world slide assets are likely necessary for the best book.
- The custom child should appear early and be celebrated as the hero of the book.
- Pose savings should be secondary to the product experience. No-pose pages should punctuate important moments, not hide the personalized child for long stretches.
- "No personalized pose" does not always mean "no art." It means the page can be built from background, environment, animal clue, POV, generic background children, or end-matter design without generating a new main-child pose.

Highest-value new pose candidates:

1. Opening park arrival: custom child visible, joyful, and clearly the hero.
2. Looking up at the slide: excitement starting to mix with uncertainty.
3. Bedtime/tangled feeling: child carrying the slide feeling home.
4. Return to top of slide: child steadying themselves, ready but not rushed, possibly holding rail.
5. Dream one-step practice: child on a bridge/edge/step, visibly practicing "one step."
6. Optional final landing joy if POV slide payoff feels emotionally insufficient.

Working recommendation: expect **3-6 new personalized child poses**, not a full new set. The increase is justified because the personalized child needs to be celebrated early and carry the emotional climax.

The shell is visually strong, but the current compressed arc already has enough clue mechanics. It should return only if it earns a specific story job.

## Current Read

We do not need all-new assets. The dream-world middle can reuse a large portion of the existing visual system.

We probably do need new assets for the real-world slide bookends, early child-hero introduction, and some dream mechanics. That is acceptable because those are the pages that now carry the story's emotional premise, personalization promise, and payoff.

The best-book path is:

- reuse existing dream assets where they genuinely fit
- generate/design new real-world slide pages
- show the custom child clearly in the first narrative pages
- rely on no-pose POV/environment pages for selected expensive moments
- add new personalized poses where child expression/action is structurally important
