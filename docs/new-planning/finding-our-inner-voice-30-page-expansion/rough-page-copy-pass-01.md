# Rough Page Copy Pass 01

## Purpose

This is a working rough-copy document for the expanded version of *Finding Our Inner Voice*.

This is not final copy. The goal is to workshop page rhythm, kid-language, emotional clarity, and page turns before moving into final copy or asset reconciliation.

Working placeholder:

- `[Child Name]` = the personalized child's name
- `[Hometown]` = the child's hometown; current default is "A cozy town"
- `[animal guide]` = the selected animal guide
- `[animal clues]` = animal-specific trail, such as pawprints, footprints, feathers, or tufts of fur
- `[subject pronoun]` = he, she, or they
- `[object pronoun]` = him, her, or them
- `[possessive pronoun]` = his, her, or their

>
> Swings squeaked.
> Sneakers thumped.
> Kids laughed and called, "Again, again!"

Story job:

Introduce the real-world park with energy, warmth, and the personalized child clearly visible as the hero.

Visual intent:

Notes:
