# Original Page Fit Audit - 2026-06-03

## Purpose

This audit evaluates the current 14-page story as raw material for the expanded 30-page version of *Finding Our Inner Voice*.

The current pages should not be treated as locked canon. Some images, poses, and ideas are useful, but the expanded story needs a stronger real-world setup, a clearer emotional arc, a better animal-guide buildup, and a more satisfying ending.

## Working Expansion Spine

The current best-fit spine:

> The child is trying to find out who the quiet voice belongs to, but along the way they have to learn how to tell the real voice apart from fear, outside noise, self-criticism, and tempting shortcuts.

The original pages should be judged by whether they help that spine.

## Overall Assessment

The current book has a strong mood and a clear theme, but it works more like a lyrical sequence than a full story. It moves from one gentle lesson to the next without enough cause/effect, suspense, discovery, or payoff.

The current assets are still valuable. The problem is not the visual world. The problem is that the current page order asks the world to carry too much meaning without enough plot.

Strongest reusable elements:

- bedtime / dream threshold
- night path and stars
- glowing door
- animal tracks
- running / joy
- shell discovery
- cave bravery
- giant flowers / wonder
- deep trail / nearing the voice
- animal reveal
- flying home

Weakest or most replaceable story functions:

- immediate dream-walk without setup
- repeated voice-as-teacher explanations
- picnic/body-listening page if it remains disconnected from the plot
- mountain/perspective page if it only says "see how far you've come"
- abrupt animal reveal
- abrupt ending

## Fit Against The New Arc

### Real-world setup

The current book has no real-world problem. This is the biggest structural gap.

The expanded version should likely begin with 2-4 pages in the child's real life before the dream-walk. Possible setup types:

- another kid asks the child to do something that feels wrong or uncertain
- the child faces a moral choice
- the child has confusing feelings after an event
- the child is afraid of something and does not know how to manage the fear
- the child hears too many outside voices and cannot tell what they think or feel

The dream-walk should then be invited by that problem, not start because the story needs to start.

### Dream threshold

Current pages 1-4 are good threshold material, but they should be reorganized:

- bedtime page becomes the transition out of the real-world problem
- night forest becomes the first clue or first listening practice
- glowing door becomes a choice
- stars become wonder and a rule of the world

These pages should feel less like the voice teaching from above and more like the child noticing, wondering, choosing, and reacting.

### Animal-guide buildup

Current page 5 is doing too much alone. The animal guide should be felt earlier and more often before the reveal:

- tracks beside the child's footprints
- a tail, feather, paw, shadow, or sound just out of sight
- a funny almost-reveal
- the animal helping without being seen
- a clue that changes based on the selected animal
- child misinterprets the guide as something outside them

The reveal on current page 13 can stay, but the story needs to earn it.

### Confusion and outside voices

No current page really handles this. This is likely one of the main new sections.

Potential fit:

- star page becomes "too many twinkly instructions"
- forest page becomes whispering leaves with competing advice
- mountain page becomes a place where echoes shout different answers
- child must learn that the true voice is usually not the loudest

This section can create child-off-page visual pages using signs, stars, wind, echo words, or path markers.

### Fear / self-criticism / warbled voice

Current cave material is the best fit.

The cave should probably become the place where the voice gets distorted:

- "I can't do this" appears first as a scary or mean-sounding thought
- the child learns to ask what the thought is trying to protect them from
- underneath the harsh voice is a smaller message: "I'm scared. Go slow."
- the cave echo returns the child's own brave words

This would make self-criticism concrete and kid-understandable.

### Trust-your-gut choice

No current page fully provides this yet.

The glowing door, trail fork, shell, or shiny shortcut could become the choice:

- one path is flashy and loud
- one path is quiet and ordinary
- the child feels a tiny "no" or "not that one"
- trusting the quieter feeling leads to progress

This would make "trust" active instead of explanatory.

### Joy and confidence

Current running and giant-flower pages are the best fit.

These should come after the child successfully listens, not before. Then joy has a cause. The child trusted the inner voice, something worked, and the body feels light.

### Reveal and bond

Current reveal page has the right idea but not enough relational payoff.

The animal guide should not only say "I am your quiet inside voice." The child should feel recognition:

- "That was you at the door?"
- "That was you in the shell?"
- "That was you when I got scared?"
- "Not exactly," the animal might say. "That was you. I helped you hear it."

The animal can become a child-friendly form of the inner voice without making the concept too abstract.

### Return and payoff

Current page 14 is a transition, not a complete ending.

The expanded version should return to the real-world setup:

- the child wakes up
- the original confusing/scary/moral situation returns or is remembered
- the child pauses, listens, and makes a small choice
- the ending shows an ongoing relationship with the inner voice

This is where the story can show the concept working outside the dream.

## Pose And Asset Implications

The existing 12 child poses are still useful. The expanded version should try to reuse them before generating new ones.

Promising child-off-page or no-new-pose page types:

- star/window close-up
- signs or path markers giving conflicting advice
- animal tracks beside child footprints
- glowing stones or shell clue
- cave echo / wall text / warbled voice visual
- empty path where the trail disappears
- animal-only near-reveal pages
- spiral breathing exercise
- About Little Hero Labs / parent prompt pages

Potential new poses to consider only after the beat map:

- child hesitating or hiding before a scary choice
- child listening with hand on heart/ear in a more explicit way
- child reaching toward animal or clue
- child speaking up or choosing in the real-world payoff
