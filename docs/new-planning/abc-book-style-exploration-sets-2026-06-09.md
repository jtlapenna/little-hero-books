## Read On First GPT Batch

The first GPT batch is attractive, but it reads more like a Finding Our Inner Voice atmospheric background than a Wonderbly-style alphabet page.

What drifted:

- Too much full-page glow and painterly atmosphere.
- Too much empty luminous scenery rather than a central action/object spot.
- Edges are soft and blended instead of clean, cartoon-like, and outlined.
- Palette is mostly warm gold/pastel, while the reference uses bolder page-by-page color fields.
- The art feels like a dreamy environment, not a printed alphabet-book page with a toy-like vignette.

What to borrow from the Wonderbly reference without copying it:

- One flat matte background color per page.
- A central spot illustration or small vignette, not a full landscape.
- Bolder character/object shapes with clean outlines.
- A lively cartoon/storybook object language, with playful scale.
- High whitespace discipline for top-left title and bottom caption.
- Minimal texture: printed-paper grain and subtle ink, not heavy gouache haze.
- Bright page-color variety across the book.

## Set 2 Prompt: Wonderbly-Adjacent Spot Art

Use this prompt after uploading the Wonderbly reference images, ideally 4-6 page photos or the contact sheet. Use them only for layout and style traits, not exact copying.

```text
I uploaded alphabet-book reference images. Use them only as a broad layout and illustration-style reference. Do not copy any exact page, character, pose, typography, object arrangement, color palette, or scene from the references.

Generate 6 separate square images, one for each prompt ID below. Do not make a collage, contact sheet, grid, or multi-panel image. Each prompt ID should produce its own separate image.

In the chat text, label each result with the prompt ID, but do not put any text, letters, numbers, signs, labels, captions, logos, or watermarks inside the images.

If you cannot generate all 6 images at once, generate as many as you can, then continue with the remaining prompt IDs in order.

SHARED STYLE AND LAYOUT PROMPT FOR ALL 6 IMAGES:

Create ONE square 1:1 children's alphabet-book background image.

Style target:
Make this closer to a premium personalized alphabet book: a flat matte colored page with a central spot illustration. Use clean playful cartoon linework, bold simple silhouettes, rounded toy-like forms, soft but clear shading, subtle printed-paper texture, and cheerful colors. The image should feel designed for a printed children's book page, not like a full fantasy landscape or cinematic background.

Important:
This is a BACKGROUND ONLY. A separate child character sprite will be composited on top later. Do not include any child, person, face, hands, body parts, mannequin, silhouette, or placeholder figure.

Composition:
- Use a mostly plain solid or softly textured background color.
- Keep the top-left title area very plain and empty.
- Keep the bottom caption area very plain and empty.
- Put the main object or scene as a compact central vignette, roughly in the middle 55 percent of the page.
- Leave a clear child placement area beside or within the vignette, with a believable ground, cloud, platform, or floor area.
- Keep the vignette smaller and simpler than a full-page environment.
- Use crisp readable shapes and a few charming decorative marks, but no text-like marks.

Hard negatives:
No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no alphabet symbols, no fake writing, no captions, no signs, no labels, no logos, no watermarks, no borders, no frames, no photorealism, no heavy painterly glow, no full-bleed landscape, no dense background, no cinematic lighting, no hazy magical atmosphere.

IMAGE 1 - abc_arch
Create a central spot illustration of a gentle magical arch where the journey begins. The arch should be rounded stone with simple vines and tiny flowers, clean outlines, and a small warm glow through the opening. Add a very simple path or ground patch below it. Leave open space just in front of or beside the arch for a child sprite. Make the rest of the page a flat cheerful color.

IMAGE 2 - abc_clouds
Create a central spot illustration of a few chunky rounded clouds arranged like soft stepping platforms. Use clean outlines and simple soft shading. Leave one obvious cloud platform where a child sprite could stand later. Keep the rest of the page a flat sky-blue or warm pastel color, with only a few tiny decorative sparkle dots if needed.

IMAGE 3 - abc_kite
Create a central spot illustration of a colorful kite floating above a simple cloud platform. The kite should have bold simple color panels and clean outlines. The string can curve through the air but must not end in a hand or cross the child placement area. Keep the rest of the page flat and uncluttered.

IMAGE 4 - abc_xylophone
Create a central spot illustration of a colorful xylophone resting on a simple floor patch. Use clean cartoon outlines, rounded wooden bars, and simple mallets placed beside the instrument, not held by anyone. Leave clear open space beside the xylophone for a seated child sprite. Keep the rest of the page flat and uncluttered.

IMAGE 5 - abc_name_bright
Create a reusable name-page spot illustration: a warm simple clearing or stage-like ground patch with one friendly glowing star-like orb above it. The child sprite will be the main subject later, so keep the art very minimal, centered, and open. The background should be a flat cheerful color with subtle printed texture.

IMAGE 6 - abc_trait_radiant
Create a central spot illustration of a simple clearing with soft warm rays of light falling onto an empty child placement area. The light should be graphic and clean, not painterly or overexposed. Include a few rounded bushes or simple plants around the sides. Keep the rest of the page flat and quiet.
```

## Set 3 Prompt: Retro Risograph Cut-Paper

Use this prompt after uploading 3-5 references that show risograph texture, limited spot-color printing, and vintage children's spot-art composition. Prefer public-domain or Creative Commons references where possible.

```text
I uploaded reference images for print texture, limited color, vintage children's spot-art composition, and cut-paper shape language. Use them only as broad medium and layout references. Do not copy any exact image, artist style, page, typography, object arrangement, character, palette, or scene.

Generate 6 separate square images, one for each prompt ID below. Do not make a collage, contact sheet, grid, or multi-panel image. Each prompt ID should produce its own separate image.

In the chat text, label each result with the prompt ID, but do not put any text, letters, numbers, signs, labels, captions, logos, or watermarks inside the images.

If you cannot generate all 6 images at once, generate as many as you can, then continue with the remaining prompt IDs in order.

SHARED STYLE AND LAYOUT PROMPT FOR ALL 6 IMAGES:

Create ONE square 1:1 children's alphabet-book background image.

Style target:
Use a retro risograph and cut-paper inspired children's book style. Make the image feel like a lovingly printed art-book page: limited 3-5 color palette, visible paper tooth, gentle ink grain, slightly imperfect color registration, flat layered shapes, chunky simplified silhouettes, playful mid-century geometry, and generous whitespace. Keep it warm, magical, and child-friendly, but more graphic and designed than painterly.

This should still live in the Little Hero Labs universe: tender, optimistic, magical, and premium. Do not make it harsh, punk, poster-like, adult, ironic, or visually noisy.

Important:
This is a BACKGROUND ONLY. A separate child character sprite will be composited on top later. Do not include any child, person, face, hands, body parts, mannequin, silhouette, or placeholder figure.

Composition:
- Use a mostly plain flat paper-color background.
- Put the main object or scene as a compact central cut-paper vignette, roughly in the middle 55 percent of the page.
- Keep the top-left title area very plain and empty.
- Keep the bottom caption area very plain and empty.
- Leave a clear child placement area beside or within the vignette, with a simple ground, cloud, platform, or floor patch.
- Use crisp, readable silhouettes and simple layered shapes.
- Use a few charming printed speckles or registration offsets, but keep them subtle.

Hard negatives:
No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no alphabet symbols, no fake writing, no captions, no signs, no labels, no logos, no watermarks, no borders, no frames, no photorealism, no watercolor wash, no heavy painterly glow, no full-bleed fantasy landscape, no dense background, no cinematic lighting, no airbrushed gradients.

IMAGE 1 - abc_arch
Create a central cut-paper spot illustration of a gentle magical arch where the journey begins. The arch should be made from rounded stone shapes and simple vine shapes layered like paper. Add a small warm flat-color glow through the opening and a simple path or ground patch below. Leave open space in front of or beside the arch for a child sprite. Use only a few cheerful riso-like colors.

IMAGE 2 - abc_clouds
Create a central cut-paper spot illustration of chunky rounded clouds arranged like soft stepping platforms. Use flat layered cloud shapes with subtle ink grain and slight registration offset. Leave one obvious cloud platform where a child sprite could stand later. Keep the rest of the page mostly blank.

IMAGE 3 - abc_kite
Create a central cut-paper spot illustration of a colorful kite floating above a simple cloud platform. The kite should have bold geometric panels, flat riso colors, and a thin curved string that does not end in a hand and does not cross the child placement area. Keep the page airy and graphic.

IMAGE 4 - abc_xylophone
Create a central cut-paper spot illustration of a colorful xylophone resting on a simple floor patch. Use rounded rectangular bars, visible print grain, simple shadows made from flat color shapes, and two mallets resting beside the instrument, not held by anyone. Leave clear open space beside it for a seated child sprite.

IMAGE 5 - abc_name_bright
Create a reusable name-page spot illustration: a simple stage-like ground patch with one friendly glowing star-orb above it, made from flat layered paper shapes and limited riso colors. The future child sprite will be the main subject, so keep the art minimal, centered, and very open.

IMAGE 6 - abc_trait_radiant
Create a central cut-paper spot illustration of a simple clearing with graphic warm rays of light falling onto an empty child placement area. Use layered flat shapes for the rays and a few rounded bushes or plants at the sides. Keep it bright but not overexposed, with plenty of plain page color.
```
