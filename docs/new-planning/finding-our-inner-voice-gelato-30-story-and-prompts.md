## Creative Direction

The expansion should feel like a better-paced version of the existing book, not a padded version. The animal trail / footsteps become the throughline:

- The hero first notices a tiny sign.
- The signs teach the rhythm: pause, breathe, listen.
- The trail appears, changes, disappears, and returns.
- The animal reveal feels earned because the guide has been present all along.
- The child discovers that the voice was inside them, not hiding somewhere outside.

The safest production rule is still: keep the existing 12 child poses. New pages should be background-only, animal-only, or use an existing pose.

## Placeholder Contract

Use these placeholders in manuscript text so the copy can become config-driven:

- `{childName}`: the child's display name.
- `{hometown}`: the opening hometown phrase, with fallback such as `a cozy town`.
- `{hometownReturn}`: return phrase, such as `to {hometown}` or `back to their cozy town`.
- `{possessive}`: `her`, `his`, or `their`.
- `{object}`: `her`, `him`, or `them`.
- `{animalDisplayName}`: display name for the selected animal.
- `{trailType}`: animal-specific trail phrase, such as `paw prints`, `feathers`, `giant footprints`, or `colorful fur`.

Avoid starting a sentence with a pronoun placeholder unless the renderer also supports capitalization.

## Image Prompt System

Use this base prompt for new backgrounds and any refreshed old backgrounds:

`Create a square children's book background illustration for Finding Our Inner Voice. The art should feel handmade, warm, clean, and timeless: soft gouache/watercolor-inspired color, controlled paper texture, rounded storybook forms, gentle linework, subtle depth, clear shape edges, and cozy magical realism. Use warm muted colors with enough contrast for print. Keep the lower third calm enough for a text box and leave open space where character or animal sprites may be composited. Do not include people, children, readable text, logos, watermarks, or a specific animal companion unless explicitly requested. Do not use photorealism, harsh contrast, muddy AI grain, busy tiny details, fake letters, or dark scary corners.`

Production rules:

- Generate 1:1 square source art.
- Prefer clean, larger shapes over dense detail.
- Keep character pages open in the lower/central foreground.
- Do not bake the custom child into any background.
- Do not bake in a tiger, owl, penguin, etc. The animal is customized and should be an overlay/sprite.
- If showing tracks, prefer abstract glowing trail marks unless a separate animal-specific overlay is planned.
- Match the refreshed art-direction goal from issue 44: sharper and smoother than the grainy first pass, still handmade.
