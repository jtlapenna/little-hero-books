- same artistic style, color harmony, and textural quality
- include stroke lines and paper textures

## Dimension Recommendation

Create new source masters at Lulu size or larger, then scale everything down for Gelato.

Recommended:

- Single-page source masters: `2625 x 2625 px` minimum, preferably larger if the tool supports it.
- Spiral source master: `5250 x 2625 px` minimum, preferably larger if the tool supports it.
- Save source masters as PNG whenever possible.
- Final Gelato single-page assets: `2457 x 2457 px`.
- Final Gelato spiral spread: `4914 x 2457 px`, then split into two `2457 x 2457 px` pages.

This keeps future Lulu compatibility open and lets every page in the Gelato version scale down consistently.

## Global Quality Rules

Apply these rules to every output:

- Background image only.
- No child, person, hands, body parts, or skin.
- No specific animal companion baked into the background.
- No readable text, fake letters, symbols, logos, watermarks, or signatures.
- Keep the lower third calm enough for a story text box.
- Keep important art away from the outer edge so it survives bleed and trim.
- Use large readable shapes instead of tiny noisy detail.
- Avoid harsh contrast, scary darkness, photorealism, plastic 3D, flat vector art, or muddy AI grain.
