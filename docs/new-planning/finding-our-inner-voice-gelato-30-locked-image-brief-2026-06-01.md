## Current Production Prompt Detail

The existing production background prompts are medium-detailed scene prompts, not tiny captions. They usually contain:

- A style anchor: "Using the uploaded playground reference image as a style guide."
- A repeated art-style clause: children's book illustration style, soft paper-like texture, warm muted palette, simplified rounded forms.
- A scene-specific description.
- Production constraints: square 1:1 format, no characters in the background.

For the new pages, match that level of specificity, but use the newer quality direction: cleaner edges, controlled paper texture, less muddy AI grain, and enough open lower area for the standard text box.

Base prompt for all new background pages:

`Using the current Finding Our Inner Voice background set as a style guide, create a square children's book background illustration. Maintain the same warm magical picture-book style: soft gouache and watercolor-inspired color, controlled handmade paper texture, simplified rounded forms, gentle linework, warm muted palette, cozy magical realism, and subtle depth. Make the image cleaner and sharper than the grainy first pass while still feeling handmade. Keep the lower third calm enough for the standard story text box. Do not include children, people, readable text, logos, watermarks, or a specific animal companion unless explicitly requested. Create the scene as a square 1:1 image.`
