 Q 
 Can my child be a dino trainer, scientist, or best friend? 
 Yes—your child's role in the story is completely customizable! During the book creation process, you can choose or describe the type of adventure you envision. If your child dreams of being a paleontologist, we can craft a story where they discover fossils and study dinosaur behavior. For animal-loving children, we can create narratives where they're a dinosaur caretaker or trainer. And if your child simply wants a dinosaur companion, we can create friendship stories where they and their dinosaur sidekick go on adventures together as equals. 
 Q 
 Can you avoid chasing/fighting scenes? 
 Completely! We understand that many parents—especially those with younger or more sensitive children—prefer stories focused on friendship, discovery, and problem-solving rather than conflict or action sequences. When creating your book, you can specify a gentle, nurturing tone, and our story generation will emphasize cooperation, helping, and peaceful adventures. Parents of anxious children particularly appreciate our ability to create dinosaur stories that feel safe and reassuring while still being exciting through exploration and discovery. 
 Q 
 What age is best for dinosaur stories? 
 Dinosaur stories from Leo Books work beautifully across our entire age range (0-10 years), but we customize the complexity, vocabulary, and themes based on your child's specific age. For toddlers (0-2 years), we create simple, repetitive dinosaur stories with bold illustrations. Preschoolers (3-4 years) get rhyming dinosaur adventures with clear emotional arcs. Early elementary children (5-6 years) receive more complex narratives involving problem-solving and dinosaur facts. Older children (7-10 years) can enjoy chapter-style dinosaur adventures with educational sidebars and more sophisticated vocabulary. 
 Q

 Yes! This is one of parents' favorite customization options. During the story creation process, you can specify your child's favorite dinosaur. Whether your child loves Velociraptors, Triceratops, Brachiosaurus, Pterodactyls, Spinosaurus, or even lesser-known species like Ankylosaurus or Parasaurolophus, we can make that dinosaur a central character. Our AI has extensive knowledge of dinosaur characteristics, so the sidekick dinosaur will behave in ways consistent with what paleontologists know about that species.

 Can the story be bedtime-calm instead of high-energy? 
 Absolutely—in fact, many parents specifically request dinosaur bedtime stories! When creating your book, you can indicate that you want a "bedtime-friendly" or "calm" version, and our story generation will adjust the pacing, energy level, and narrative arc accordingly. Bedtime dinosaur stories feature gentle, predictable plots, soothing illustrations with softer colors and twilight/moonlight settings, and comforting resolutions where everyone is safe and happy by the end.

You can also include family pets by name, and we'll incorporate them into the dinosaur world (imagine your dog becoming friends with a Stegosaurus, or your cat riding on a Pterodactyl's back!).

 We strive for scientific grounding while maintaining story magic.
