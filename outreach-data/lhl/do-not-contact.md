# LHL Do-Not-Contact List

Hard exclusions. Skill must check this list before drafting outreach for any creator. Never override without explicit Jeff instruction.

This is separate from (and stricter than) the 90-day frequency cap in `pipeline.md`. Entries here are permanent unless Jeff says otherwise.

## Format

```
## @[handle] — [Reason category]

**Added:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Reason:** [specific reason — declined, hostile to brand DMs, conflict of interest, child-privacy red flag, etc.]
**Channel applies to:** [all / email only / DM only]
**Override allowed?:** [Y/N — if Y, what conditions]
```

## Reason categories

- **declined** — creator politely declined; respect the no
- **hostile** — publicly complained about brand DMs / cold outreach
- **conflict** — works for/with a competitor or has structural conflict
- **employee** — LHL team member or contractor (no need to pitch)
- **partner** — existing partner via another channel (not appropriate to cold-pitch)
- **privacy-risk** — content shows child-identifying details (school, address, full name) — too risky for partnership use rights
- **out-of-scope** — wrong audience / wrong demographic / outside geo
- **bounce** — email bounced (hard bounce, address invalid)
- **opt_out** — explicit unsubscribe / "remove me" / "do not contact me again"
- **wrong_contact** — email or DM reached the wrong person (e.g., manager who didn't appreciate the pitch)
- **brand_misalignment** — discovered post-pitch that the creator's content is incompatible with LHL brand
- **other** — describe in the entry
